About This Website

Welcome to the GPA & CGPA Calculator! 🎓

This website is designed to help college and university students calculate their GPA and CGPA quickly and accurately. You can enter your subjects, credits, and grades, and get your GPA along with a motivational quote to keep you inspired.

Our goal is to make academic calculations easy, interactive, and motivating for students. No account or personal information is required — simply enter your grades and see your results.

We hope this tool helps you track your performance and stay motivated throughout your studies. Good luck! 💪
